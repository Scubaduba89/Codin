Nobody types full paths. You press Tab and the shell finishes the
word; press Tab twice and it shows the options. This sandbox is built
out of names nobody would ever type.

1. There's a deep chain of folders starting at
   `expedition-supply-depot-alpha`. Travel to the very bottom - type
   just the first few letters of each folder name and hit Tab:

       cd exp<Tab>/nor<Tab>/...

2. At the bottom you'll find a folder called `basecamp`. Plant your
   flag in it - `touch` creates an empty file:

       touch made-it.txt

3. `python3 codin.py check terminal-a1-03`

One trip down, zero names typed in full. From now on, Tab is a
reflex: it's faster AND it can't make typos.
